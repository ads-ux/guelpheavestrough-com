When to Book Gutter Cleaning in Guelph

Guelph's tree canopy decides the schedule. Late May to June is maple-key season — the winged seeds pack into eavestroughs and downspout elbows faster than leaves do, and they sprout if left wet. Late October to November, after the leaves are down and before the first hard freeze, is the cleaning that matters most: a trough full of wet leaves going into winter is how ice dams start.

Signs you are overdue: water sheeting over the front edge in moderate rain, plants growing out of the trough, staining down the siding, or a downspout that drips instead of runs. If water is still pooling at the foundation after a cleaning, the problem is more likely pitch or a damaged section — see our eavestrough repair services.

Cleaning, Guards or New Eavestrough?

Cleaning twice a year is normal. Cleaning three or four times a year means quality MicroMesh gutter guards will likely pay for themselves. And if the eavestrough is over 20 years old, leaking at several joints or sagging along the run, cleaning only buys time — a seamless replacement is worth pricing.
