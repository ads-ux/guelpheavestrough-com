Are Gutter Guards Worth It in Guelph?

Guelph is one of the most heavily treed cities in Ontario, and that is what drives the decision. Maple keys pack into eavestroughs in late spring, leaves arrive in October and November, and pines drop needles all year. A home that needs eavestrough cleaning three or four times a year is a strong candidate for guards; a home with no trees overhead often is not.

Guards also make sense on two-storey homes and steep roofs, where every cleaning visit is a bigger job. They are not a fix for a leaking or sagging system — if water is spilling over a section that has lost its pitch, that needs eavestrough repair first.
